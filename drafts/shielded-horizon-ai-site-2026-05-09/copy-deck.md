## Process

1. Discovery and eligibility signals

Collect basic employer context, workforce signals, current benefit posture, and the questions the team wants answered.

2. Document and data checklist

The AI Assisted workflow prepares a missing-item map for payroll, census, benefits, and stakeholder review needs without exposing sensitive data in public reports.

3. Education brief and review packet

The employer receives plain-language context and a controlled path for next-step review, including what is known, unknown, and pending.

4. Human-reviewed implementation path

Any estimate, claim, recommendation, or implementation step is moved through human review before it becomes final.

## FAQ

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. The site is educational. Employers should review their specific situation with qualified legal, tax, payroll, benefits, and plan-administration professionals.

What does the AI actually do?

AI helps organize intake, draft education materials, track missing items, prepare approval packets, and keep next actions visible. It does not make final compliance decisions.

Does Shielded Horizon replace our health plan?

The review is designed to understand how a Section 125-related path may fit with the employer's existing structure. Fit depends on the employer's facts and review results.

What information is needed for a review?

Basic workforce, payroll, benefits, and stakeholder context are needed first. Sensitive materials should only be provided through approved secure channels.
